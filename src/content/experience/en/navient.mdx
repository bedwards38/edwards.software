## Correspondence Services - IT Intern
*Navient | May 2022 - May 2024*

For roughly two years, I was apart of the Correspondence Services team at Navient, where I was a crucial asset to the development of new enterprise, omni-channel communication applications. 

Unlike the typical internship, I would continue to work for Navient part-time throughout the school year. This unique opportunity enabled me to take more ownership over the systems I'd develop/contribute to; compared to most interns, who usually only spend 10 - 12 weeks with an organization.

### Responsibilities  
Though my title was officially "IT Intern", the expectations for my role were more equivalent to that of a full-stack engineer or a SWE I. I played a pivotal role in significantly enhancing the enterprise's B2C communication infrastructure by designing and implementing comprehensive correspondence software solutions from scratch. 

I co-led the development of three mission-critical services: Enterprise Email 2.0, Template Studio, and Campaign Center. Across these applications, I was responsible for designing intuitive user interfaces, architecting multi-threaded backend logic, and developing normalized database schemas -- a comprehensive skillset that enabled me to drive significant improvements in user experience and system performance.

My involvement in the development of these initiatives from conception to deployment proved invaluable, enabling me to rapidly accelerate my technical expertise compared to what I would have gained from schooling alone.

### Initiatives
> Since these are all proprietary systems belonging to Navient, I cannot discuss any specific design or implementation details

#### Enterprise Email 2.0
The first half of my time at Navient was spent on the redesign and implementation of the enterprise's B2C emailing system, Enterprise Email 2.0, using tools like Spring Boot, Twilio Sendgrid, and Microsoft SQL Server. This solution enabled the enterprise's different business units to quickly and seamlessly contact customers through email regarding password resets, loan payment status/reminders, etc. 

The redesigned system was developed using a microservice arcitecture, open-source libraries, and an in-house application framework, Nucleus. The initial production release of Enterprise Email 2.0 occurred in February 2023, saving the business from over $75k/year in licensing costs accrued from the legacy Enterprise Email service.

After the successful relese of Enterprise Email 2.0, my focus shifted to two interconnected services that would work to expand the capabilities of the new Enterprise Email:

##### Template Studio
Template Studio was a custom web application that enabled business users to desgin custom email templates with a user-friendly drag-and-drop interface. Using a set of pre-defined content blocks, similar to components in a web framework, business users could build out a template in mere minutes, compared to the hours that were spent previously. 

Each template created in the studio was complete with robust version control and dynamic variable substitution logic tailored to the enterprise's needs.

##### Campaign Center
Campaign Center was developed in tandem with Template Studio, serving as the backend engine for sending mass email campaigns to customers using pre-designed templates created in Template Studio.  

One of the key challenges we faced during development was implementing compliance with the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), which regulates acceptable hours for sending emails to consumers. To address this, we implemented a complex delay logic system that would automatically detect and defer any emails that were being sent outside of "acceptable" hours, ensuring seamless communication while maintaining regulatory adherence.

#### IT Talent Review System 
Over the summer of 2023, I led a team of junior engineers in developing Navient's IT Talent Review System, a critical tool the enterprise uses for evaluating and growing technical talent. Building on an existing Excel-based system, we designed and implemented a web service that enabled IT managers to provide annual reviews and feedback to employees. 

As the project lead, I was responsible for guiding the development team through multiple iterations, starting with an initial implementation in Java and Spring Boot. After we delivered the first version of the application and the rest of the interns left at the end of the summer, I refactored the codebase to C#/.NET to ensure a smooth transition for the succeeding app team which preferred those technologies to Java.
