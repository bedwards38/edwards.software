## Band Boosters Association Website
<Subtitle content="Completed: Sep 2024" repo={frontmatter.repo} images={frontmatter.images} galleryTitle={frontmatter.title}/>

This was a proof-of-concept (POC), created to help a band boosters association (BBA) modernize their website. 

> Since this is an archived project, there is no published demo to explore. Feel free to check out the images or the codebase from the completed POC, instead!  

### Design
The site was built with Astro 4, Tailwind CSS, Preline UI.

The design of the site came from an open-source template, intended to be used for a business; however, I altered the design of the template to be more aligned with the BBA's existing site. 

Major changes to the template included: 
- Page structure/styling
- Component design/function/styling
- Support for multiple languages

### Background
One afternoon, I recieved a text from my sister, a band director, asking if I would be interested in helping her school's BBA modernize their website for the 2025 school year. Being the kind brother that I am, I agreed to the request and had a working POC in a little under a day. 

The main goal I had with this POC was to demonstrate my ability to quickly "bootstrap" together a working site, *not* to sell the BBA on a redesigned site. Due to restrictions set by employment contract with Edgile, I was unable to assist in any official capacity; however, I saw this as an excellent chance to expand my skills in both web dev and requirements gathering.

After I had achieved my goal, I began conceptualize additional features I thought the BBA might find useful:
- Integration with a payment processor to allow the BBA to accept donations or student dues online
- Integration with various calendar services to enable seamlessly communication and updates to parents
- Implementation of a "simple" user account system with role based access control

While I shared the initial design with my sister, I made the decision to halt further development in early October 2024. I'd accomplished my goal of creating a functional POC, and there wasn't much value to be gained by continuing to invest my time into the project. 

The BBA made the smart decision to partner with an established organization to design, implement, and manage their redesigned site for the 2025 school year. In the end, I was happy with my ability to put together a working prototype of a product with a clear vision for expansion.
