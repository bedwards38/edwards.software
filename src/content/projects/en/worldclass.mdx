## Worldclass
<Subtitle content="Contribution Timeframe: Sep 2023 - May 2024" demo={frontmatter.demo} />

To satisfy our senior capstone requirement, my team (consisting of 4 RHIT CSSE students) chose to work alongside the founders of a small startup -- [Worldclass](https://worldclass.cc) -- a customizable, all-in-one software for your business or community. 

> I am unable to discuss specifics regarding the product's design. My contributions to the company and its product ended after I graduated in May 2024.

### Background
Worldclass is a web app -- built with Next.js, PostgreSQL, GraphQL and Docker at the time of my contributions. 

My team was actively involved with new feature conceptualization/development, discussions regarding the direction of the product, and decisions regarding product-market-fit. This gave me unique insight into how a startup operates. Most importantly, it taught me the benefits and challenges that come with a role where function/responsibilities aren't clearly defined. 

Through it all, I found I *really* enjoyed working with that level of freedom, collaboration, and indeterminism...

### Contributions
My team's initial focus was to expand the product into new verticals by establishing connections with local businesses. While the product did get some positive reception, we were greatly limited by the low number of small businesses that existed in the Terre Haute area at the time. We transitioned to be more involved with product design and feature development to better support business toward the middle of the school year. 

The founders decided to shift the product's direction from being a payments/scheduling system to an all-in-one ecosystem for small businesses/clubs/communities. This led to a sizeable increase in the number of feature requests from existing clients.

Specific features I contributed to during this period include:
- Configurable integration framework enabling users to integrate and exchange data between Worldclass and external services/platforms
- QR Scan-to-Pay for kiosk systems that quickly genenerates a QR code that redirects customers to pre-populated shopping carts
- Payment refund processing system allowing businesses to process and track customer refunds, with automatic updates to product inventory

The founders developed the product on MacOS, but the RHIT-mandated laptops ran Windows. We all didn't have easy access machines running MacOS, but I found that I had no problem running the project on my Linux desktop. Through some trial and error, I got the codebase running on my Windows laptop using WSL, so I also created a developer setup guide to share with my team and the team that took over once we graduated.
