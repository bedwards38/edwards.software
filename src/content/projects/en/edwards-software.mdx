## edwards.software
<Subtitle content="Published: Apr 2026" repo={frontmatter.repo} />

The site you're currently on acts as my "portfolio", "curriculam vitae", "extended-form resume", or whatever else you'd like to call it. Simply put, I just wanted a place to showcase my personal projects and professional experience to anyone that might be interested: family, friends, colleagues, recruiters, hiring managers, you get the gist.

### Current Design
This site is built using Astro 6 and Typescript, and its hosted on Cloudflare. Since I'm partial to a good terminal emulator -- Arch Linux user here -- I designed the site to visually mimic a terminal/command line interface.  

To speed up development, I chose to start building the site from a <a href="https://github.com/686f6c61/Portfolio-Astro-Template/tree/6313417a73d5e35fa03335080cc6e1988496cc03">template designed by 686f6c61</a>. It was somewhat visually close to what I was envisioning for my portfolio, so I felt it would be the right choice; however, soon after beginning development, I realized it wasn't designed to be as modifiable as I'd hoped (no disrespect towards 686f6c61, code styles/preferences tend to differ greatly between developers). 

To achieve the visual design you see today, I rewrote the entire source code of the template. At the time of publishing, very few elements remain from the original template that could be recognized in this site, but I feel it's important to still provide attribution to the template's original creator for their work.

I heavily reworked the internal design to better align with the standards I typically set for my projects: highly maintainable, easily understandable, and consistent with modern design philsophies. 

Specific changes to highlight include:
 - Component-driven design
 - Mobile-first/responsive styling
 - Markdown-based content specification
 - Expanded terminal-inspired design language 
 
### Background
This has, technically, gone through many iterations since early 2022. At one point, I built a "functional" prototype of a portfolio using raw HTML, CSS, and JS -- no frameworks, just the stuff that made sites from the 90s and early 2000s purr like an '02 Mercury Sable.

My senior year of college, I wanted to get more "in-tune" with the state of modern web dev for my capstone project. I scrapped my original design, chose an open-source template to save some time, and built up a pretty functional porfolio using React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. I even published the site using Vercel under a free domain, but it never felt complete.

Toward the end of that school-year, I discovered <a href="https://astro.build">Astro</a>: a new, emerging framework geared for smaller sites like the portfolio I wanted to build. I quickly became hooked and (poorly) rebuilt my site using Astro's React integration. I broke a lot of things I liked about the template I chose, and told myself I'd get around to fixing them later... we all know how that goes though... I did get around to acquiring the <i>edwards.software</i> domain about a year later, in 2025.

In early 2026, I decided it was time to get my sh** together (for lack of a better term). I realized that most of the things I thought were "cool" about the first template weren't the best to include with a simple portfolio site. I did a lot of research into different frameworks and debated between a few before settling back on Astro (mainly due to my prior experience writing functional Astro apps, though I came really close to choosing Svelte). I may rewrite the site now using SvelteKit since I finally have a functional, published site... stay tuned!
